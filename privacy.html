Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

In short: this website doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and doesn't collect or store anything you type into it. We only receive personal information when you choose to contact us by email or phone, and we use it to answer you and to look after your vehicle.

Who we are

This policy covers the website of Art's Autobody & Collision Centre, 2407 A Kaladar Ave, Ottawa, Ontario K1V 8B9 ("we", "us"). It explains what personal information we receive through this website, why, and what we do with it.

Our owner, Yasir, is responsible for how we handle personal information. You can reach him using the details under Contact us about your information.

What information we receive

Information you choose to send us

The estimate form on our home page does not send anything to our website, and the website doesn't store it. When you press the button, the form opens a new email in your own email app, addressed to yasir@artsautobody.ca. Nothing reaches us unless you send that email.

If you do contact us by email or phone, we receive what you choose to share, which usually includes:

  • your name, and your phone number and/or email address
  • your vehicle's year, make and model
  • a description of the damage and the service you're interested in
  • any photos you attach

Photos can show more than the damage, such as licence plates, people or places, and may contain location data added by your phone. Please only send what you're comfortable sharing.

Please don't send payment card numbers, banking details, or copies of your driver's licence or other ID by email.

The damage estimator

The interactive damage estimator runs entirely in your web browser. Your selections aren't sent to us or anyone else, and they aren't saved. If you use "Add report to my estimate request", your selections are copied into the email form on the same page, and you decide whether to send them.

Technical information handled by our web host

Like any website, this site is delivered to your browser by a web hosting service. As a normal part of doing this, the hosting service's servers may automatically record technical information about each request, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the date and time. This is used to deliver the site, keep it running and protect it from abuse. We don't use it to identify visitors or to build profiles of anyone.

What we don't collect

  • We don't use cookies or similar tracking technologies. See our Cookie Policy.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising pixels, social media plugins or embedded third-party content (such as embedded maps or videos).
  • All fonts, scripts, images and the 3D model are served from our own website, so loading a page doesn't send your information to other companies.
  • There are no user accounts, and we don't sell or rent personal information.

Why we collect it and how we use it

We use the information you send us only to:

  • reply to your question or estimate request;
  • arrange a consultation or inspection, and prepare an estimate;
  • carry out and follow up on repair work you ask us to do;
  • keep business records we're required to keep, such as invoices for tax purposes.

We don't use your information for marketing unless you ask us to keep in touch.

By contacting us, you consent to us using your information for these purposes. You can withdraw that consent at any time (see below), although we may then be unable to provide an estimate or complete work, and we may still need to keep some records where the law requires it.

Who we may share it with

We only share personal information when it's needed for the purposes above:

  • Service providers that we use to run our business, such as our email and telephone providers, which handle messages on our behalf.
  • Your insurance company, if you ask us to deal with them about a claim for your repair.
  • Parts suppliers, which may receive vehicle details (such as make, model, year or VIN) when we need to order parts for your repair.
  • Authorities, where we're required to share information by law.

Some of our service providers may store or process information outside Ontario or outside Canada, where it may be subject to the laws of those places.

Links to other websites

Our site links to Google Maps (for directions and to read our reviews). Nothing is loaded from Google until you click one of those links. Once you're on Google's site, Google's own privacy policy applies.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes above. As a general practice:

  • enquiries that don't lead to work are deleted once they're no longer needed to respond to you;
  • information about repair work, such as estimates and invoices, is kept for as long as we need it to stand behind the work and to meet our legal and tax record-keeping obligations, and is then securely deleted or destroyed.

How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information, including:

  • keeping the website simple: it has no forms that submit data to it, no database of visitor information and no user accounts;
  • limiting access to our business email and records to the people who need it to serve you;
  • using password-protected accounts and devices for our business email;
  • deleting information we no longer need.

No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure. Ordinary email isn't encrypted end to end, which is why we ask you not to send payment or ID details by email. If you prefer, call us or share details in person at the shop.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and how we've used it;
  • give you access to it;
  • correct it if it's inaccurate or incomplete;
  • stop using it, or delete it, where we're not required to keep it.

We'll respond as soon as we reasonably can. We may need to confirm your identity before sharing or changing information. If we can't grant a request, for example because the law requires us to keep a record, we'll explain why.

If you have a concern about how we've handled your information, please contact us first so we can try to fix it. You can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, which oversees private-sector privacy in Ontario.

Children

This website is intended for adults arranging vehicle repairs. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact us about your information

Changes to this policy

If we change how this website handles information, for example by adding a new service, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date before the change takes effect.
