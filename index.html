The process

Anatomy of a repair.

  1. 01

    Strip & inspect

    When it's needed, panels come off so hidden damage behind them isn't missed.

  2. 02

    Metal & dent work

    Dents are pulled back to factory body lines. Damaged parts are repaired, or replaced when repair isn't the right call.

  3. 03

    Paint & colour match

    Digitally matched colour, booth-sprayed base coat and clear coat, blended so the repair disappears.

  4. 04

    Reassemble & inspect

    Every panel refitted, gaps and alignment checked in full light. Then the keys go back to you.

Services

Every panel. Every finish.

Everything happens under one roof on Kaladar Ave, from the first look at the damage to the final polish.

  1. 01

    Collision repair

    From a fender bender to heavy body damage, restored to pre-accident condition. Bumpers, fenders, doors and quarter panels repaired, or replaced when repair isn't the right call.

  2. 02

    Dent removal

    Dings, creases and hail marks reshaped back to the panel's original lines. Factory paint preserved wherever possible.

  3. 03

    Paint & colour matching

    We read your paint code and account for fade, then mask, prime and booth-spray, blending so the repair can't be seen.

  4. 04

    Free consultations

    A straight answer on what the repair involves, what it should cost and whether insurance makes sense. With owner Yasir, hands-on, no pressure.

How it works

Four steps. No guesswork.

  1. 01

    Consult

    Stop by or call ahead. We look at the damage with you and explain what needs doing, free.

  2. 02

    Plan

    You get a written estimate. Parts, paint code and timeline are confirmed with you before work starts.

  3. 03

    Repair

    Dents pulled, panels repaired or replaced, then masked, primed and sprayed in the booth.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    We inspect the finish in full light, check every gap and edge, then hand you the keys.

About us

We treat your car like ours.

Art's Autobody & Collision Centre has more than 30 years of auto body and collision experience. Our team is driven by care: for your vehicle, and for the people who drive it.

Owner Yasir runs the shop hands-on, so you deal directly with someone who knows your car, your repair and your timeline. From a small dent to a van the insurer wanted to write off, every job gets the same attention to detail.
