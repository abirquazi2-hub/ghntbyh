Estimate requests and contacting us

Sending us a message, a damage report or an estimate request through this website, by email or by phone doesn't create a contract between you and us, and doesn't authorize us to do any work. It's a request for information or an appointment.

If you'd like us to repair your vehicle, the details are set out separately, in writing, before work begins. That includes:

  • the work to be done, and the parts and materials to be used;
  • the estimated price, and how and when payment is due;
  • the expected timeline;
  • any warranty on parts and workmanship;
  • your authorization for the work, and how any changes to it will be approved.

Those documents, such as a written estimate, a work authorization and an invoice, govern the repair. If anything on this website seems to conflict with them, the written documents for your repair apply.
