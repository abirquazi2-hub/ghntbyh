Planning first

A plan built around your household, not a product.

Westdale works through a structured planning process: gathering information, running planning calculations, researching products where appropriate, and presenting customized proposals for your review.

Planning continues after implementation, with ongoing service for clients. Explore the process, the areas we advise on, or go straight to a conversation with Steve.

Retirement Income & Tax Planning

What it addresses
How retirement income is structured and how tax considerations shape it, before and after you stop working.
May be relevant to
People preparing for retirement, approaching it, or already retired.
The planning involves
Gathering your information, running planning calculations and tests, and reviewing a customized proposal together.

Investment Funds, Plans & Products

What it addresses
Researching and selecting investment funds, plans and products where appropriate to your situation.
May be relevant to
People building savings, reviewing an existing portfolio, or comparing the cost of advice.
The planning involves
Product research where appropriate, then a customized proposal for your review. Investments carry risk and values can go down as well as up.
Related Westdale programs
Portfolio Recovery Strategy Program · Portfolio Recovery Report · Fee for Service Calculator

Risk Management & Insurance

What it addresses
Protecting your household and your plan against the financial effects of unexpected events, through insurance where appropriate.
May be relevant to
Families, business owners and anyone whose plan depends on continued income or capital.
The planning involves
Reviewing needs within your overall plan, researching products where appropriate, and presenting a customized proposal.
Related Westdale programs
Life Insurance Capital Management System

Severance & Pension Transfers

What it addresses
Decisions that arise when leaving an employer: how to treat a severance payment or a pension transfer.
May be relevant to
People facing a job change, retirement from an employer, or a pension commuted value decision.
The planning involves
Analysis of your options inside your whole financial picture before a decision is made, and a customized proposal.
Related Westdale programs
Commuted Value Proposal System
Discuss a pension or severance decision

Inheritance & Wealth Transfers

What it addresses
Receiving, managing and passing on wealth: how an inheritance fits your plan, and how your estate can be organized.
May be relevant to
People who have received or expect an inheritance, and those planning their own legacy.
The planning involves
Reviewing your circumstances and goals and preparing a customized proposal. Westdale does not replace legal advice on wills or estates.
Related Westdale programs
Estate Capital Management System
