Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This website doesn't use cookies. It also doesn't use analytics, advertising or social media trackers, or similar tracking technologies. That's why you won't see a cookie banner here.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website can store in your browser. Similar technologies include browser storage (such as "local storage"), tracking pixels and device fingerprinting. They can be used to make a site work, to remember preferences, or to track what people do across sites.

What this website uses

  • No cookies. Our pages don't set any cookies, first-party or third-party.
  • No browser storage. The damage estimator, the estimate form and the rest of the site don't save anything in your browser. If you reload the page, your selections are gone.
  • No analytics or advertising tools. We don't use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, advertising networks, reCAPTCHA, or embedded maps, videos or social media posts.
  • No third-party requests when a page loads. Fonts, scripts, images and the 3D model all come from our own website.

Technically necessary technologies

Like any website, our site relies on standard web technology to work. Your browser may keep copies of our pages, images and files in its ordinary cache so they load faster next time. This is handled by your browser, doesn't identify you, and you can clear it in your browser settings at any time.

Our web hosting service receives standard technical information, such as your IP address, when your browser requests a page. See "Technical information handled by our web host" in our Privacy Policy.

Links to other websites

Our site links to Google Maps for directions and to read our reviews. These links don't load anything from Google until you click them. Once you're on Google's website, Google's own cookie and privacy policies apply.

If this changes

If we ever decide to add cookies or similar technologies that aren't strictly necessary, such as analytics, we'll update this policy first. Where consent is required, we'll ask for it before any of those technologies are used, and you'll be able to say no.
